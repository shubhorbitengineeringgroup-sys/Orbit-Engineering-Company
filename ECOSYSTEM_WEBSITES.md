## Owner confirmation required

- **Central India Pvt Ltd:** supplied logo only identifies CENTRAL INDIA PVT LTD. Similar manufacturers and EPC companies have different legal names/locations. No working official website can be tied confidently to this logo. Do not substitute cieco.in or ciepl.com from an unrelated or uncertain namesake.
- **OM Construction:** supplied logo reads OMCC / From concept to Creation. Legal/rating records suggest Om Construction Companies India Private Limited, Tikamgarh, but no official public website was verified. Do not substitute other similarly named contractors.
- **Measurement Solutions:** supplied measurement.png explicitly reads **Measurement Specialties**. The verified site for that logo is [TE Connectivity's MEAS brand page](https://www.te.com/en/products/brands/meas.html); [TE's acquisition announcement](https://investors.te.com/news-releases/press-release-details/2014/TE-Connectivity-Completes-Acquisition-of-Measurement-Specialties-Inc/default.aspx) confirms ownership. Owner clarification is required because the supplied display name/scope describes another identity.

These three records retain their existing profile behavior and have no guessed redirect. An owner clarification question is pending.

## Reachability notes

Some official government sites block automated readers or occasionally return a service/captcha error. Their identities were checked using primary government references; this is not a claim of uninterrupted availability. SBEM's older sbem-india.com URL failed live certificate validation, so the current sbeminstruments.com site is used. No search-result or third-party business-directory URL is used as an organisation's website.
