## Responsible competitor-query approach

Build a visible, evidence-based procurement guide first. If a named comparison is later useful to customers, verify both providers' current public offerings, state the comparison date, distinguish known facts from unknowns, cite original sources and give customers a fair selection framework. Do not imply endorsement or affiliation or assert better pricing, certification, outcomes or delivery times without proof.

Keep this competitor list out of site-wide titles, metadata, image alt text, `sameAs`, client lists and hidden text. Publish client or equipment-brand material only in the context of a genuine documented relationship. Google's policies caution against keyword stuffing, misleading content and low-value pages created for query manipulation. [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

**Hindi pointer:** competitor ka naam bharne se uske har search par apni site top nahi aayegi. Unke buyers ke real questions ka achha jawab, apna verified project proof aur clear local service information zyada useful foundation hai.

After publication, monitor relevant service queries and qualified enquiries, then improve pages with demand. Repeat the competitor review periodically because addresses, sites and services can change. Account setup and deployment checks are in [SEO rollout](SEO-ROLLOUT.md).
